# Food category definitions

Version 2026-11-16. **183 categories** (`CATEGORIES.md`): 172 USDA WWEIA + 11 additions (X01–X11). These definitions are the instructions for the AI that will assign one category to every product. Borderline foods follow USDA's own placements where USDA has them; deviations are marked *(differs from USDA)*. Machine-readable copy: `tools/categories/categories.json`. Edit `tools/categories/definitions.py` and re-run `python3 -I tools/categories/build_definitions.py`.

## Rules for every product

1. **One category.** Every product gets exactly one category: the one that best describes what the product **is**, as sold (packaged foods) or as eaten (dishes).
2. **Processing is a tag.** Fresh, frozen, canned, dried, raw or cooked do **not** change the category (frozen broccoli → 6407, canned peaches → 6008). Record the state as a tag. Exception: dried fruit has its own category (6016).
3. **Uncooked staples.** Dry or uncooked staples go to the category of the food they become: dry pasta → 4004, dry rice → 4002, dried lentils → 2802, rolled oats → 4802.
4. **Ready meals and kits.** Ready meals, frozen meals and meal kits go to the dish they make: frozen pizza → 3602, lasagna → 3204, taco kit → 3502, risotto kit → 3202.
5. **Single food vs dish.** A single food with only seasoning, marinade or sauce stays in its own category (marinated chicken breast → 2202, tuna in oil → 2402). Combined with other foods (pasta, rice, vegetables, bread) it becomes a dish (31xx–38xx).
6. **Sandwiches and wraps.** Sandwiches, wraps and subs go to the 37xx category of their **main filling**.
7. **Use the nutrition values.** Some categories split on nutrients; use the product's values per 100 g / 100 ml. **Milk fat**: whole ≥ 3.0 g, reduced fat 1.8–2.9 g, lowfat 0.6–1.7 g (incl. European semi-skimmed 1.5 %), nonfat < 0.6 g; unknown → reduced fat. **Ready-to-eat cereal**: sugars > 21.2 g → 4602, otherwise 4604. **Diet drinks** (71xx): ≤ 1 g sugars per 100 ml and sweetened with non-nutritive sweeteners ("zero", "diet", "light", "sugar-free").
8. **Biscuit.** "Biscuit" means a cookie in UK/European usage (→ 5504) but a bread-like quick bread in US usage (→ 4402). Decide from the product, not the word.
9. **Plant-based alternatives.** Milk alternatives → 1902, yogurt alternatives → 1904, meat / cheese / other alternatives → 2806, ice cream alternatives → 5802 (differs from USDA).
10. **Survey-only categories.** 6489 *Vegetables on a sandwich*, 7702 *Tap water* and 9602 *Human milk* exist only for diet surveys. **Never** assign them to a product.
11. **Last resort.** 9999 *Not included in a food category* only for non-food items (pet food, cosmetics, cutlery) or a food that truly fits no definition. Target: < 1 % of products.

## Milk & dairy (15)

- **1002 Milk, whole**: Plain whole milk (≥ 3.0 g fat per 100 ml).<br>*Includes:* whole cow's or goat's milk, lactose-free whole milk, evaporated milk, whole milk powder, buttermilk.<br>*Not:* flavoured milk → 1202; plant-based drinks → 1902.<br>*USDA examples:* Milk, whole · Milk, lactose free · Milk, evaporated · Milk, dry · Buttermilk · Goat milk
- **1004 Milk, reduced fat**: Plain reduced-fat milk (1.8–2.9 g fat, US "2 %"). Default when the fat level is unknown.<br>*Includes:* 2 % milk, lactose-free reduced-fat milk.<br>*Not:* flavoured → 1204.<br>*USDA examples:* Milk, reduced fat (2%) · Milk, lactose free · Milk, NFS · Milk, evaporated
- **1006 Milk, lowfat**: Plain lowfat milk (0.6–1.7 g fat), incl. European semi-skimmed (1.5 %).<br>*Includes:* 1 % milk, semi-skimmed milk, kefir (USDA places kefir here).<br>*Not:* flavoured → 1206; drinking yogurt → 1820.<br>*USDA examples:* Milk, low fat (1%) · Kefir · Milk, lactose free
- **1008 Milk, nonfat**: Plain nonfat / skimmed milk (< 0.6 g fat).<br>*Includes:* skim milk, fat-free milk, nonfat dry milk.<br>*Not:* flavoured → 1208.<br>*USDA examples:* Milk, fat free (skim) · Milk, lactose free · Milk, dry · Milk, evaporated
- **1202 Flavored milk, whole**: Sweetened or flavoured milk made with whole milk.<br>*Includes:* chocolate milk, strawberry milk, sweetened condensed milk, prepared hot chocolate with whole milk.<br>*Not:* milkshakes → 1402; cocoa powder → X09.<br>*USDA examples:* Chocolate milk · Milk, condensed · Strawberry milk
- **1204 Flavored milk, reduced fat**: Flavoured milk made with reduced-fat milk (or fat level unknown).<br>*Includes:* chocolate or strawberry milk drinks, malted milk, prepared hot chocolate.<br>*Not:* milkshakes → 1402.<br>*USDA examples:* Chocolate milk · Hot chocolate / cocoa · Strawberry milk · Milk, malted
- **1206 Flavored milk, lowfat**: Flavoured milk made with lowfat / semi-skimmed milk.<br>*Includes:* lowfat chocolate milk, flavoured semi-skimmed milk drinks.<br>*Not:* milkshakes → 1402.<br>*USDA examples:* Chocolate milk · Hot chocolate / cocoa · Strawberry milk
- **1208 Flavored milk, nonfat**: Flavoured milk made with nonfat milk.<br>*Includes:* fat-free chocolate milk, nonfat hot cocoa.<br>*Not:* milkshakes → 1402.<br>*USDA examples:* Hot chocolate / cocoa · Chocolate milk · Strawberry milk
- **1402 Milk shakes and other dairy drinks**: Milkshakes and other ready dairy drinks that are not plain or flavoured milk.<br>*Includes:* milkshakes (fast food, bottled), eggnog, ice cream sodas, dairy dessert drinks.<br>*Not:* drinking yogurt → 1820; protein / meal-replacement shakes → 7208; coffee drinks → 7302.<br>*USDA examples:* Milk shake, fast food · Milk shake, home recipe · Eggnog · Chocolate milk drink · Ice cream soda · Milk shake with malt
- **1602 Cheese**: Cheese of any kind except fresh curd cheeses and cream cheese.<br>*Includes:* cheddar, mozzarella, feta, parmesan, gouda, halloumi, brie, blue cheese, cheese slices and strings, grated cheese, processed cheese and cheese spreads.<br>*Not:* cottage cheese, ricotta → 1604; cream cheese → 8006; plant-based cheese → 2806; cheese dips and sauces → 8412.<br>*USDA examples:* Cheese, NFS · Cheese, Cheddar · Cheese, Mozzarella · Cheese, Parmesan · Cheese, American · Cheese, Feta
- **1604 Cottage/ricotta cheese**: Fresh curd cheeses.<br>*Includes:* cottage cheese, ricotta, quark, fresh white cheese (queso fresco, queso blanco).<br>*Not:* cream cheese → 8006.<br>*USDA examples:* Cheese, cottage · Puerto Rican white cheese · Cheese, Ricotta · Cottage cheese · Cheese, cottage cheese
- **1820 Yogurt, regular**: Yogurt that is not strained (regular).<br>*Includes:* plain, flavoured or fruit yogurt, drinkable yogurt, yogurt tubes, yogurt with toppings or granola.<br>*Not:* Greek / strained / skyr → 1822; frozen yogurt → 5802; plant-based → 1904; baby yogurt → 9010.<br>*USDA examples:* Yogurt, low fat milk · Yogurt tube · Yogurt, whole milk · Yogurt, liquid · Yogurt, nonfat milk · Yogurt, NS as to type of milk
- **1822 Yogurt, Greek**: Strained, high-protein yogurt.<br>*Includes:* Greek and Greek-style yogurt, skyr, labneh, strained yogurt (plain or flavoured).<br>*Not:* regular yogurt → 1820.<br>*USDA examples:* Yogurt, Greek
- **1902 Plant-based milk**: Plant-based milk alternatives for drinking or cereal.<br>*Includes:* almond, oat, soy, rice, coconut and pea drinks (sweetened, unsweetened, flavoured, barista), coconut milk.<br>*Not:* plant-based cooking cream → 8008; plant-based yogurt → 1904.<br>*USDA examples:* Almond milk, unsweetened · Oat milk · Almond milk, sweetened · Soy milk, unsweetened · Soy milk, sweetened · Coconut milk
- **1904 Plant-based yogurt**: Plant-based yogurt alternatives.<br>*Includes:* soy, coconut, almond and oat yogurt alternatives.<br>*Not:* plant-based milk → 1902.<br>*USDA examples:* Yogurt, almond milk · Yogurt, coconut milk · Yogurt, soy

- **8406 Mustard and other condiments**: Mustard and other condiments (narrowed: vinegar is X05).<br>*Includes:* mustard, hot sauce, sriracha, horseradish, wasabi, lemon or lime juice used as a condiment, sweet and sour sauce, chutney.<br>*Not:* vinegar → X05; relish → 8408.<br>*USDA examples:* Mustard · Hot pepper sauce · Lemon juice, 100% · Vinegar · Fry sauce · Sweet and sour sauce
- **8408 Olives, pickles, pickled vegetables**: Olives, pickles and pickled vegetables.<br>*Includes:* olives, pickles and gherkins, relish, sauerkraut, kimchi, capers, pickled peppers and beets, marinated vegetables in jars.<br>*USDA examples:* Pickles, NFS · Olives, NFS · Pickles, dill · Olives, black · Relish, pickle · Beets, pickled

- **X05 Vinegar**: Vinegar.<br>*Includes:* all vinegars (wine, apple cider, balsamic, rice), balsamic glaze.<br>*Not:* dressings → 8012.
